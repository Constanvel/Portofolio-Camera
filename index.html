about

Constantine Rainer Simanjuntak — PPLG, SMK Telkom Purwokerto. I build for the web with React, Tailwind and Node — a reading and essay platform, an art community, my school’s own site, a spending tracker — and AI tools on top of open models. Finalist in the UI/UX Design competition at Sevent 9.0, Telkom University Purwokerto. Off the clock I make aesthetic desktop videos for TikTok, usually to something shoegaze.

Built with borrowed parts, listed here because they were borrowed — every one of them under a licence that allows it. The photographs, the writing and the code are mine.

  • 3d · ipodiPod Classic by Timothy Ahene — CC BY 4.0, textures halved and recompressed.
  • typeVCR OSD Mono by Riciery Leal. Everything else is the system's own UI face.
  • music“Backbay Lounge” by Kevin MacLeod — CC BY 4.0 — a 48-second loop cut on the bar, re-encoded quieter and smaller.
  • soundShutter snap generated with ElevenLabs, trimmed to its 200-millisecond click.
